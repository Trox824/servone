## On an upstream merge

- Keep the `navModelExtender` export and the call at the end of `buildNavModel`.
  If `buildNavModel` is renamed or restructured, call the extender wherever the
  final `NavigatorModel` is returned.
- Keep the `inboxNotificationFilter` export and the filter in the `inboxNotifications`
  derived store (with `contextById` as an input). If the store is restructured, filter
  wherever the merged list of inbox notifications is built.
- Keep the `servoneTeam` guard at the top of both NodeContent files and the chip branch
  in ObjectNode.
- Keep the `chunter:class:Channel` branch at the top of `canArchiveSpace` and the
  permission check at the top of `UnarchiveChannel`.
- Keep `isArchivedType` checks wherever card-resources decides which types can be created
  (TypeSelector, `isBaseTypeWithSubtypes`, `getFirstCreatableSubtype`, CreateCardButton,
  NavigatorType). If upstream adds another create path, add the check there too.
- Keep `ApprovalGuardMiddleware` in the pipeline before `MarkDerivedEntryMiddleware`, and the
  `MODEL_VERSION` env override in `common/scripts/esbuild.js`.
- Keep the WaitingApproval branch in the REST tx route of `pods/server/src/rpc.ts`.
- Keep `reminders` out of the diff `onEventUpdate` copies to other participants' Event copies.
