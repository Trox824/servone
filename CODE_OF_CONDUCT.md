# Code of Conduct

Servone follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, discussions, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email **contact@servone.app**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may warn, suspend or ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
